Bever is a free, open-source note application that keeps everything on your device: notes, attachments, and the search index. There is no account, no telemetry, no advertising, and no application server.

<b>Notes</b>
• Notebooks, notebook stacks, tags, shortcuts, and a trash
• Rich text, tables, checklists, code blocks, and links between notes
• Pictures, audio, video, and PDF pages shown in the note
• Annotations on pictures, and sketches that stay editable stroke by stroke

<b>Search</b>
• Offline full-text search with operators such as tag:, notebook:, and created:
• Finds text inside pictures and marks it where it stands
• Optional on-device text recognition; nothing is sent anywhere

<b>Your notes are yours</b>
• Imports and exports ENEX files without loss, so a library kept in Evernote can be brought along
• Optional synchronization through your own pCloud storage, started by you. pCloud is a proprietary service, and the remote copy is not end-to-end encrypted

Bever is an independent project and is not affiliated with Evernote or pCloud.
